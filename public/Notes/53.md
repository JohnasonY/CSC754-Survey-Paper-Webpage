# A cellular automata approach for modelling pedestrian-vehicle mixed traffic flow in urban city

1. Main idea: Develop a unified cellular automata model for mixed pedestrian-vehicle traffic, focusing on how pedestrians entering vehicle lanes affect traffic flow and safety.

2. New approach: Combine a multi-grid spatial representation, an improved KKW vehicle model with dynamic acceleration and synchronization distance, and a TTC-based pedestrian movement model.

3. Simulations: Compare IKKW with KKW-1, validate pedestrian motion against experimental data, and examine pedestrian intrusion, traffic phases, speed limits, and sidewalk widths.

4. Main results: Pedestrian intrusion reduces vehicle speeds, creates uneven traffic across lanes, and produces six distinct mixed-traffic phases. The peak simulated conflict frequency at 60.48 km/h is approximately 3.5 times that at 30.24 km/h, while wider sidewalks reduce conflicts in certain pedestrian-density ranges.
